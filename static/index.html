Collection Hardware

  • Gateway & Array: Ecowitt GW3900 Wi-Fi Gateway (integrated indoor temp, humidity & barometer probe) paired with wireless outdoor weather (WS69 and WH32) and air quality sensors (WH45)
  • Measured Telemetry: Outdoor temperature, humidity, dew point, barometric pressure, wind speed/direction/gusts, rainfall, UV/solar radiation, CO₂, and PM10
  • Weather Cam: Ecowitt HP10 WittCam (IP66 weatherproof, 150° wide-angle) capturing 5-minute snapshots and daily timelapses
  • Telemetry Pipeline: WeeWX logging 5-minute archives and daily summaries to MySQL, served by FastAPI behind Apache & Cloudflare Tunnel

Local Area — Ferny Grove

15 km NW of Brisbane CBD ~85 m Elevation D'Aguilar Range Foothills Upper Kedron Brook

Located in Brisbane's outer north-west against the eastern foothills of the D'Aguilar Range and D'Aguilar National Park, Ferny Grove sits in the undulating upper catchment of Kedron Brook. The surrounding hills and creek valleys create a greener, slightly cooler microclimate than the inner city, experiencing a humid subtropical pattern with warm, storm-active summers, afternoon north-easterly sea breezes, and crisp, mostly dry winters.
